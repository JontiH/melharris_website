How we work

A clear process, start to finish.

  1. Consultation

    We meet at your home to talk about how you live, what you love and what isn't working, and agree on scope and budget.

  2. Concept

    Floor plans, mood boards and a palette of materials, so you can see where the design is going before anything is ordered.

  3. Design

    Detailed drawings, finishes, lighting and a furniture plan with every piece specified and priced.

  4. Installation

    We manage orders, trades and deliveries, then style the finished rooms so they're ready to live in.
